Appearance: Deep orange-red broth with a thin layer of red oil. Rice noodles pale white, toppings laid on top. Looks warming.


Broth: Savoury on first sip, heat rises gradually, sustained but not fierce. Two more sips and numbness surfaces on the tongue and inner lips, like a faint current. Medium-high heat, medium numbness. Not greasy, not cloying.


Noodles: Medium thickness, slippery with a slight bounce. Ordered "no bean sprouts, no vegetables", so the bowl is almost all noodles.


Toppings:
- Pig's ears: crunchy and chewy, an interesting contrast to the noodles.
- Pork belly: fatty and lean layered, melts in the mouth, savoury.
- White radish: soft and tender, soaks up the spicy broth, sweet and refreshing, balances the richness.


Overall: Numbness, heat, saltiness and umami are well balanced. Finished with a light sweat and numb lips.
