When I eat rice noodles, I usually order them spicy, because I love spicy food. How spicy I get them usually depends on my appetite and mood that day. Also, when I eat rice noodles, I just want to eat more noodles, so I always ask for "no bean sprouts and no vegetables." That way, half of my portion won't turn out to be bean sprouts or vegetables instead of noodles. The toppings I choose are really just a few kinds. If I absolutely have to eat vegetables, I'll go for white radish. As for meat, since one of this shop's signature toppings is pork belly, I sometimes order that too. For more unusual toppings, I'll have pig's blood or pig's ears; sometimes I might add a cuttlefish ball as well. Given my appetite, two to three toppings are already very filling and quite satisfying.
